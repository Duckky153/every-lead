A tool that sorts a day of small-business insurance leads: who to call now, what to fix first, and what a person reads first. Good leads don't wait behind broken ones.

100 leads came in today. 81 go now, 21 of them with a question for the first call. 13 get fixed first and 6 wait for a person. None are dropped.

Jev, an AI model from TypeSafe, reads each note. Plain code decides the rest. Try it: open a Fix first lead, type in the missing detail, and watch the check run again.

All made up. We gave 40 leads problems on purpose to test the checks. This says nothing about real leads.
